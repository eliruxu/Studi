# Studi

> **Find your focus. Share the journey.**

Studi is a social study-spot discovery app for college students. It helps students find comfortable, productive places to work on and around campus while connecting with friends and learning from other students’ experiences.

## Highlights

- **Explore nearby spots**: Browse study spaces by distance or ratings from other students.
- **Use the map**: See nearby study spots, tap into them, and get a better sense of what each place is like.
- **Find your ideal setup**: Filter for things such as noise level and amenities.
- **Get the full picture**: Read reviews, check tags, see how busy a spot is, and find out whether friends have been there recently.
- **Track a study session**: Check in, use the built-in timer, and check out when you finish.
- **Share your experience**: Rate a spot for quietness and overall experience, then leave a short review.
- **Keep up with friends**: Follow their activity and build study streaks together.

## Why Studi?

Starting college often means navigating an unfamiliar campus and city while trying to find a reliable place to focus. Inspired by the way people use **beli** to discover and rate restaurants, Studi brings that same community-driven idea to one of the most important parts of student life: finding a good place to study.

We created Studi to help students in the Boston, Cambridge, and MIT area explore new spaces, know what to expect before they arrive, and share the places that help them do their best work.

## How It Works

1. **Explore** study spots near you through a list or interactive map.
2. **Compare** locations using ratings, reviews, busyness, amenities, and noise level.
3. **Filter** results to match your preferences.
4. **Check in** to a spot and use the study timer to track your session.
5. **Rate and review** your experience to help the next student.
6. **Connect** with friends and stay motivated through shared activity and streaks.

## Challenges & Growth

One of our biggest challenges was building a map that made it easy to compare study spots at a glance. Our early versions did not feel accurate or intuitive enough, so we brought in Mapbox to create a clearer, more polished experience. We also worked through showing a user’s current location in the simulator and letting people sort spots by proximity or rating.

This was our team’s first hackathon and our first mobile app. In 24 hours, we learned how to turn an idea into a working iOS app, use new tools, and improve the experience through testing and conversation.

## What’s Next

- Expand beyond Boston and MIT to college communities everywhere.
- Improve search and personalization with more study-spot data and user reviews.
- Add a conversational AI assistant for choosing where to study.
- Enable richer social interaction, including comments and messaging.
- Let students set session goals, such as problem sets, reading, papers, or lectures, when checking in.
